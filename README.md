# Casa Livre — low-poly house planner

A lightweight, offline 3D home-planning sandbox for Android and Windows. Developed for Arthur Henrique Tostes Batista Aranha.

## First playable version (0.1)

- **Lot setup:** width and depth in meters, rural or urban horizon, flat or mountainous scenery, grass/tall grass/dirt/concrete surface, optional perimeter wall with configurable height and finish.
- **Terrain:** paint individual 1 m² squares, use custom solid colors, or place a stylized 2 m pitanga tree.
- **Walls:** place one-meter wall courses on grid lines; rotate their alignment and raise or lower one wall or every wall.
- **Windows and doors:** attach editable-size openings to a wall while it is low. Transparent indicators stay visible at their planned height. Once raised, the wall includes the opening.
- **Roofs:** select two corners to generate an adjustable-pitch gable roof or a shed roof over a rectangle. Several separate roof sections can be combined for larger footprints.
- **Furniture:** sink, table, bed, sofa, and cabinet, with editable width, depth, height, and solid color.
- **Paint:** choose any color and apply it to a specific side of a wall, furniture block, or roof panel. Door and window recesses are separately paintable.
- **Save:** project stored locally on the device and automatically offered for loading on the title screen.

All geometry is generated by the engine. There are no textures, downloaded assets, shadows, accounts, ads, or network services. The project uses Godot 4's Compatibility renderer to favor older graphics hardware.

## How to play

1. Choose the lot dimensions, landscape, starting ground, and perimeter wall, then press **START BUILDING**.
2. Select a tool from the top-left dropdown.
3. Move the camera to aim the central crosshair at the desired place; press **PLACE**.
4. Use **Save project** after building.

**Windows:** WASD or arrow keys move the target; hold right mouse button and drag to orbit the camera; scroll to zoom; left click places; `R` rotates the upcoming piece; Delete removes the aimed piece.

**Android:** drag the open 3D view to orbit; use the direction buttons to move the camera target; tap **PLACE** to place or apply a tool. The design is optimized for landscape orientation.

## Downloads

Every push to `main` starts GitHub Actions builds for:

- `CasaLivre-Windows` (a `.exe`, no installation required).
- `CasaLivre-Android` (a debug-signed `.apk`, for manual installation).

Open the repository's **Actions** tab, select the latest successful **Build Android and Windows** run and download the matching artifact. GitHub may package it as a ZIP containing the executable/APK.

These CI-generated binaries are prototypes and should be tested before distribution. The Android debug signature is for testing only, not publishing to Google Play.

## Development

- Engine: Godot **4.7.2**, standard GDScript, `gl_compatibility` renderer.
- Open `project.godot` with Godot 4.7.2.
- Test core logic: `godot --headless --script res://tests/smoke.gd`.
- Export presets: `Windows Desktop` and `Android`.
- Scene entry: `scenes/Main.tscn`.
- 3D geometry and paintable box primitives: `scripts/ShapeFactory.gd`.
- Game, world, tools, save/load, and interface: `scripts/Main.gd`.

## Known limits / next iterations

- Current roof pieces cover **rectangles**; there is no automatic roof intersection or complex polygon trimming yet.
- No undo/redo, mouse-picked camera target, multi-storey construction, or independent vertex editing yet.
- The optional gray-block perimeter is currently represented by a gray solid color, not masonry joint geometry.
- Individual objects use simple block geometry. Dimensions are approximate planning aids, **not architectural or structural plans**.
- Compatibility renderer still needs graphics hardware with OpenGL ES 3 on Android. Performance must be measured on the intended oldest test devices; universal compatibility cannot be guaranteed.
